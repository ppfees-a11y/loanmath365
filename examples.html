Worked loan examples

Illustrative U.S. dollar scenarios, not lender quotes.

Example 1: $25,000 over five years at 8%

At 8% nominal annual interest, a $25,000 loan repaid in 60 equal monthly installments costs approximately $506.91 per month. Total scheduled interest is about $5,414.60 before fees. Minor cent differences can arise from rounding. Try the calculator with these same inputs to see every month.

Example 2: A lower payment may cost more

Consider a $20,000 loan at 9% for 36 months versus 60 months. The longer term reduces the monthly obligation but increases the number of interest-bearing months. Use the comparison tool to quantify the trade-off for your budget.

Example 3: A separate origination fee

For a $10,000 loan, a $300 fee paid upfront adds $300 to the total cash outflow. If the lender deducts the fee from proceeds, you might receive only $9,700 while still owing payments based on $10,000. Our fee tool assumes the fee is separate, not financed.

Example 4: Paying extra

If you pay $100 more each month toward principal, the loan balance may reach zero earlier. The benefit depends on the outstanding balance, rate and timing. Verify that your lender accepts additional principal without penalties.
