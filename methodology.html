Methodology and calculation assumptions

Transparent formulas make estimates easier to verify.

Fixed monthly payment

Let P be original principal, r be nominal annual interest divided by 12 and then by 100, and n be the number of monthly payments. Payment M = P × r / [1 − (1 + r)−n]. For zero interest, M = P / n. Terms are converted from years to the nearest whole number of months.

Amortization schedule

For each month, interest = outstanding balance × r; principal paid = payment − interest; new balance = prior balance − principal paid. The final installment is limited to the amount needed to pay off the remaining balance. Values display to cents but internal calculations retain additional precision.

Extra principal payments

Each month we calculate interest on the outstanding balance, then apply the scheduled payment plus the specified extra amount to principal, stopping when the loan is paid off. Savings compare total interest with and without extra payments.

Payment budget and DTI

Affordable principal is the inverse of the fixed-payment formula. Debt-to-income ratio equals monthly debt payments divided by gross monthly income, multiplied by 100. The tool displays existing and projected DTI separately.

Important limitations

Results exclude taxes, insurance, compounding conventions other than monthly, variable rates, grace periods, daily accrual, penalties and financing of fees. A loan offer’s legal APR requires additional assumptions and disclosures not modeled here. Lender payment rounding may differ.

How the fee-adjusted effective annual rate is estimated

For a fee deducted from loan proceeds, the borrower receives principal minus fee but makes the scheduled principal-and-interest payments. We solve for the monthly discount rate that equates those payments to net proceeds, then compound that monthly rate over 12 months: (1 + monthly rate)12 − 1. This is an effective annual yield estimate, not the legal APR calculated under Regulation Z. Actual APR treatment depends on which charges are finance charges, timing, and applicable disclosures.

Extra-payment scenarios assume each additional amount is applied to principal at the monthly payment date, annual additions occur every 12th payment, and the one-time amount is added in the selected month. No prepayment penalties are modeled. All calculations are educational estimates.
