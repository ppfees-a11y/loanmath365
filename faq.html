Loan calculator frequently asked questions

Does this calculator show the lender’s APR?

No. Enter the nominal annual interest rate. A lender’s disclosed APR can reflect certain finance charges and may differ.

Can I download the entire payment schedule?

Yes. Calculate a loan on the home page, then choose Download schedule CSV. The file can be opened in spreadsheet software.

Are the tools free?

Yes. No registration or subscription is required.

Do extra payments always save money?

Extra principal often reduces interest on a conventional amortizing loan, but contract terms and prepayment penalties matter. Check with your lender.

Why does my bank show a different final payment?

Lenders may calculate daily interest, use different payment dates or rounding rules, and include fees or insurance.

Does DTI determine approval?

No. DTI is only one factor; lenders also evaluate credit, income documentation, loan type and other criteria.

Does the site provide loan offers?

No. LoanMath365 is an independent educational calculator site, not a lender or broker.

Can I compare loans with different terms?

Yes. Compare payment, interest and separate fees. The lowest monthly payment may not be the lowest total cost.
