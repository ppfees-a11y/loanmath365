About LoanMath365

LoanMath365 provides independent, free loan calculation tools and plain-language borrowing education for U.S. visitors. Created by James Gore.

Our purpose is to make loan payment mathematics, amortization, fees, and borrowing trade-offs understandable without asking for personal financial information.

We are not a lender, broker, credit counselor, or financial adviser. We do not originate loans or guarantee qualification, rates, savings or approvals.

We periodically review examples and calculation logic. For calculation details, see Methodology. For usage limitations, see Terms.
